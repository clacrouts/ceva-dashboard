Yearly position of the shoreline (metres, seaward up), relative to its average; the band is ± one standard error, the line the trend.

What to keep in mind

Positions are satellite waterlines moved to mean sea level with the tide and the waves of each image. The standard error measures how precise a yearly position is, not how accurate: against surveys at six validation beaches, yearly positions were 8 to 39 m from the surveyed ones (root mean square). A trend over a few metres a year, or over many years, is more reliable than any single year.
